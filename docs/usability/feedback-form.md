# Feedback questions (ask after the SUS, write the answers down)

1. In your own words, what is this app for?
2. What was the most confusing moment today? What did you expect to happen?
3. Was there anything you looked for and could not find?
4. Which part would you actually use when studying? Which part would you never use?
5. Did any word or label in the app not make sense?
6. (Phone) Was anything hard to reach or too small to tap? (Laptop) Did you want a keyboard shortcut anywhere?
7. If you could change one thing, what would it be?
8. Anything else?

Record: device, browser, screen size, and whether the participant uses a screen reader, larger text, or dark mode.

## What this session covers that the automated tests cannot
- Whether people understand what the app is for and what the labels mean.
- Whether they can find features without being told.
- How it feels with a real screen reader (VoiceOver, TalkBack): run tasks 1-3 once with one switched on.
- Satisfaction (the SUS score).
